Why this course exists

Removing the barriers between people and their medicine

Project ADHERE encourages medication and vaccine adherence in underserved immigrant communities through culturally appropriate education, accessible resources, and community-partnered events. Our goal is to build trust in healthcare and improve health outcomes for people who face language barriers, cost, and distrust of the medical system.

Too often, treatments that could keep people healthy go unused, not because the medicine doesn't work, but because of the barriers standing in the way. This course prepares you to help remove those barriers.

The project is run by a team of undergraduate students at the University of Pennsylvania, and this course was written for middle and high school students who are curious about public health. No prior background is needed.

How the course works

Four parts to your experience

By the end, you'll understand what public health is, how diseases and medicines work, why medication adherence is so hard, and how social conditions shape health. You'll also be able to communicate all of it clearly and respectfully to the people who need it most.

◧

Lectures

Each of the seven modules has a slide deck that walks you through the core concepts, with real examples and data.

✦

Readings

Every module includes three short readings from trusted sources and a video to deepen what you learn in the lecture.

✎

Capstone

Design a real public health flyer on a topic of your choice. The strongest flyers may be used in ADHERE's community materials.

✓

Final quiz

A short quiz at the end helps you check your understanding and reinforce the key ideas before moving on.

One submission, at the very end

All course materials live on this website. There is only one thing to submit: a single final form containing your capstone project, the quiz, and a course reflection. Submitting that form marks the end of the course.
